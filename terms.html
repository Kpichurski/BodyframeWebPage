1. What Bodyframe is

Bodyframe is an app for iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android that helps you keep a short list of daily habits for a set number of days, and lets a small circle of friends see your progress and cheer you on. You choose a program, tick your rituals off each day, and can attach a photo if you want to.

Bodyframe is a habit and consistency app. It is not a health, medical or fitness service. Nothing in the app is medical, dietary, psychological or fitness advice, and it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional. The recipes in the Kitchen and the movement ideas in The Edit are general suggestions, not a prescription. Bodyframe does not collect health or fitness data and makes no claim about any outcome for your body or your health.

The programs are generic templates. They have not been reviewed for your circumstances, and some of them ask for things — a restricted diet, a set volume of water, two workouts a day, an earlier wake-up — that are not right for everyone. Please speak to a doctor or another qualified professional before you start, and especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, are under 18, have a medical condition, take medication, or have any history of disordered eating. Adapt or skip any ritual that is not right for you, stop if you feel unwell, and take part entirely at your own risk.

Recipes are general inspiration, not dietary advice — always check the ingredients yourself for anything you are allergic or intolerant to. If you are struggling with your relationship with food, exercise or your body, please talk to a health professional or a support organisation where you live.

5. Challenges, rules and restarts

Each program has its own character, and some are written to be strict. The Hard 75 states the rule “miss a day, start over” — that is the ethos of that program and a promise you make to yourself. Bodyframe does not reset your challenge for you. Pausing, resuming and restarting a challenge are always your choice, in every program, and you can customise the habit list, the length and the start date at any time.

6. Your circle

Friends are added with an eight-character invite code that you share. There is no public directory and no user search. Anyone you connect with can see your first name, your program, your day number, which rituals you have ticked and when, and the daily photo you share. Only share your invite code with people you want to see that.

Bodyframe has no chat, no direct messages and no comments. Encouragement is a single cheer, once per friend per day.
