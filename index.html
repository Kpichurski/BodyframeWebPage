Nº 02

How it works

Four tabs — Today, Kitchen, Circle, You. Almost everything happens on the first one, and ticking your rituals off takes seconds.

  1. I

    Begin in a minute

    Enter a first name, answer three questions, choose a program, pick its length and a start date. That is the whole setup. There is no login, no password, no email and no social account to connect.

  2. II

    Do your rituals

    Four or five small things, listed for the day. Nothing to plan, nothing to score. The Kitchen tab keeps 107 recipes across Mornings, Lunch, Snacks and Dinners for the days you need an idea, and you can bookmark the keepers.

  3. III

    Mark them off

    Tap each ritual as you finish it and Bodyframe keeps the time you did it. Attach a photo as proof if you want to, and add a daily selfie. Current streak, longest streak and completion rate sit quietly behind it all.

The whole sign-up

No login. No password. No email. A first name and a start date.

Reminders are daily. It works offline — tick a ritual with no signal and it syncs later. Two home-screen widgets keep your progress, and a friend’s, on the outside of the app.

Nº 03

Your circle

A circle is not an audience. It is a handful of people who know what you said you’d do today, and who are quietly glad when you do it.

Added by invitation
Friends join your circle with an eight-character invite code you give them. There is no public directory and no user search.
Her day, as it happens
See which day she is on, which rituals she has ticked, and the time she ticked them.
Cheering
One tap sends her encouragement and lands on her phone. Once per friend, per day — enough to mean something.
Glimpses
A Stories-style look at a friend’s photo of the day, with “seen by” and a one-tap light.
The Edit
A daily editorial screen: an affirmation, a journal prompt, a recipe of the day, a movement idea and a mood image. The journal is private and never shared.
Discover
Public community groups with a shared photo feed, for when your circle wants to be a little wider.

Nº 05

Questions

Every answer is open — nothing to click, nothing hidden.

Is Bodyframe free?

Yes. Bodyframe is free right now, and nothing in it is locked behind a paywall.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login, no password, no email and no social account. You enter a first name, answer a three-question quiz, choose a program, and pick its length and start date.

Is there an Android app?

Yes — Bodyframe is on Google Play as well as the App Store. On iPhone it needs iOS 17 or later.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing automatic. “Miss a day, start over” is The Hard 75’s stated rule — the ethos you sign up to. The app never resets your challenge for you; pausing, resuming and restarting are always things you choose.

How do friends find me?

Through an eight-character invite code you share with them. There is no public directory and no user search.

Can we message each other?

No. Bodyframe has no chat, no direct messages and no comments. Encouragement is one tap — a cheer, once per friend per day, which arrives on her phone.

Can I change the habits?

Yes. You can customise the habit list in any program, and choose your own length and start date.

Does it track health data?

No health or fitness data of any kind is collected — no HealthKit, no weight, no measurements, no calorie tracking. Your photos and progress sync to Bodyframe’s own server.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Bodyframe is offline-first. Tick your rituals with no connection and they sync when you are back.

Is the journal shared?

No. The daily journal prompt in The Edit is private and is never shared with your circle.
